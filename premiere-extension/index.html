CastCut

Multicam editing, right inside Premiere

1
Open the sequence with your cameras — each camera's video on its own track (V1, V2, V3…), each mic on an audio track.
2
Tap Read sequence. Your cameras appear below.
3
Name each camera, choose Solo / Wide / Duo, and pick its mic.
4
Tap Create multicam edit. CastCut cuts between angles automatically. Works on a duplicate by default — ⌘Z undoes.
