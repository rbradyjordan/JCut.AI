# Music Videos & Performance

> Music videos, performance/live cuts, lyric-driven pieces. The song is law.
> Builds on `kb/fundamentals.md`, `kb/recap-videos.md`, `kb/pacing-and-rhythm.md`.

## The song is the edit

`analyze-music` first — BPM, beats, downbeats, sections are the entire structure.
**Every cut is beat-motivated.** Cutting off the beat in a music video reads as a
mistake unless it's deliberate syncopation for effect.

- **Cut on the beat; big shot changes on downbeats; structural changes on section
  boundaries** (verse → chorus → bridge).
- **Energy follows the song.** Verses can breathe with longer takes; choruses get
  tighter, busier, more dynamic; the bridge/breakdown shifts the visual approach.
- **Lip-sync must be frame-accurate** when performance is on camera. Align the
  performance clip so mouth matches the track precisely; this overrides other timing.

## Techniques

- **Multiple performances/angles** intercut on the beat keep energy up; rotate angles.
- **Speed ramps** on instrumental hits and transitions; **beat-synced** punch-ins.
- **Hard cuts dominate;** flashes/whips on big hits sparingly.
- **Visual motifs** that recur on the hook tie the piece together.
- **Performance vs. narrative/B-roll:** cut to narrative/concept footage in verses,
  return to performance for choruses (or vice versa) — establish a pattern, then play.

## Self-review

- [ ] Is every cut on a beat; big changes on downbeats?
- [ ] Is lip-sync frame-accurate where performance is on screen?
- [ ] Does visual energy track the song's sections (verse vs chorus)?
- [ ] Angle/shot variety on every beat-cut (no repeats)?
- [ ] Does the hook get a recurring visual motif?
