# Documentary & Narrative

> Story-first, longer-form: docs, mini-docs, brand stories, YouTube narrative.
> Builds on `kb/fundamentals.md` and `kb/interviews-dialogue.md`.

## Story is the spine

Structure beats footage quality. Find the arc: **setup → tension/conflict →
turn → resolution.** A story with a clear emotional throughline holds attention
even with imperfect footage; gorgeous footage with no story does not.

- **Build from interviews/VO** (the radio edit — see `kb/interviews-dialogue.md`).
  The spoken narrative carries the film; everything else illustrates it.
- **Show, then tell (or tell, then show).** Pair each idea in the audio with footage
  that illustrates or deepens it — never just literal B-roll of the words.
- **Sequences, not shots.** Build mini-sequences (establish → detail → reaction →
  result) that move the story a step forward.

## Pacing & breathing room

Documentary lives on **earned pauses**. Hold on a face after a hard admission. Let
ambient sound and a wide shot breathe between chapters. The audience needs time to
*feel*. (See breathing-room timings in `kb/interviews-dialogue.md` — docs use the
longer end of the range.) Vary it: tighten exposition, open up emotional beats.

## Techniques

- **Cold open** with the most compelling moment/question, then back up to set context.
- **Chapter structure** for longer pieces; signal transitions with a held shot,
  ambience, music shift, or title.
- **Music underscores emotion** but ducks well under narration; let scenes play dry
  (no music) sometimes — silence is a tool.
- **Archival/stills:** add slow push-ins (Ken Burns) for life; cut on narration beats.
- **Lower-thirds** to name speakers on first appearance (caption, brief, on a held beat).

## Self-review

- [ ] Is there a clear arc (setup → tension → turn → resolution)?
- [ ] Does the cold open hook with a real question/moment?
- [ ] Are emotional beats given room to land (not cut away instantly)?
- [ ] Does footage illustrate/deepen the audio, not just literally match it?
- [ ] Music supports without burying narration; scenes breathe where needed?
- [ ] Speakers named on first appearance?
