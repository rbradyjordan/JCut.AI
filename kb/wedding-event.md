# Weddings & Events

> Weddings, parties, conferences, performances. Emotion + chronology + the moments
> that matter to real people. Builds on `kb/fundamentals.md` and `kb/recap-videos.md`.

## What matters

The client relives a real day. **The key moments are non-negotiable** and must be
present and well-cut, even at the expense of "cool":
- Wedding: the look/first sight, vows, ring exchange, kiss, first dance, key toasts,
  parent moments, exit. Reactions matter as much as the action.
- Event: the headline moment, key speakers, crowd energy, the climax.

## Deliverables (often two)

1. **Highlight film / recap (1–4 min):** music-driven, emotional, the best of the
   day. Build it like a recap (`kb/recap-videos.md`) but lead with *emotion over
   energy* — hold on tears, laughs, glances. Pace to music but leave breathing room
   on the big emotional beats.
2. **Full-length / documentary cut:** ceremony and speeches in full, lightly trimmed,
   clean audio, chronological. Story-first (`kb/documentary-narrative.md`).

## Techniques

- **Audio is precious.** Vows, speeches, the officiant — use the best mic source,
  keep it clean and prioritized. Let key lines play; duck music under them.
- **Real reactions over staged shots.** A guest's genuine laugh beats a perfect pan.
- **Chronology with emotional logic.** Mostly follow the day's order, but lead the
  highlight film with a strong emotional hook regardless of when it happened.
- **Breathing room on emotion.** Hold the kiss, the tear, the embrace. Don't rush
  the moments people will rewatch. (Longer holds — see `kb/interviews-dialogue.md`.)
- **Color match** across the day's changing light (golden hour → reception). Adjacent
  cuts should match temperature.

## Self-review

- [ ] Are all the must-have moments present and well-cut?
- [ ] Is the key audio (vows/speeches) clean and prioritized?
- [ ] Do emotional beats breathe (held, not rushed)?
- [ ] Does the highlight open on a strong emotional hook?
- [ ] Color consistent across changing light?
