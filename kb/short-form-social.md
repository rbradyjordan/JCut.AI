# Short-Form & Social (TikTok / Reels / Shorts / Ads)

> Vertical 9:16, sub-60s, retention-obsessed. Builds on `kb/fundamentals.md`.

## The retention game

Social lives or dies on the **first 1–2 seconds** and on never giving the viewer a
reason to swipe. Every second must justify the next.

- **Hook in frame 1.** Open mid-action, on the payoff, or with a bold visual/claim.
  No intros, no logos first, no slow ramps. The strongest moment goes FIRST, even if
  it "spoils" — curiosity is what retains.
- **No dead air.** Cut every pause, breath, and filler tighter than you would for
  long-form. Pacing skews fast (but still vary it — see breathing room in
  `kb/interviews-dialogue.md`; even social needs the occasional beat to land a point).
- **Pattern interrupts** every few seconds: a cut, a zoom/punch-in, a caption pop, a
  B-roll insert, a sound effect. Keep the visual changing.
- **Captions are mandatory.** Most watch muted. Big, legible, word-or-phrase timed,
  high on screen (avoid the bottom UI zone). Keep to 1–2 lines.

## Format

- **9:16 vertical (1080×1920).** Reframe landscape sources: scale to fill, then use
  subject tracking to keep the subject centered (see framing in `fundamentals`).
- Keep critical action out of the top ~15% and bottom ~20% (platform UI overlaps).
- Loud, punchy audio; trending sounds where appropriate; music ducked under VO.

## Structure (ad / value content)

`Hook (0–2s) → Promise/setup → Value/payoff (rapid) → CTA.` State the CTA clearly;
end on it. For story content: hook → escalation → twist/payoff → button.
