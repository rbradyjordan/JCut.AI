# Trailers & Hype Edits

> Promo trailers, launch videos, announcement hype. Builds on `kb/fundamentals.md`
> and `kb/recap-videos.md` (shares the beat-driven, escalation DNA).

## The trailer arc

Trailers sell a feeling and a question, not the whole story. Classic three-act build:

1. **Cold open / hook** — atmosphere or a striking image; establish tone. Often slower.
2. **The build** — introduce the world/stakes/product; cuts tighten; tension rises;
   music swells. Use **rising risers** and **pre-drop silence** (a beat of near-silence
   before the drop hits harder).
3. **The drop / montage** — the fastest, most intense section: rapid beat-synced cuts
   of the best/biggest moments. This is the payoff.
4. **The button** — after the montage, a hard cut to black, then ONE final beat: the
   title card, logo, date, or a last punchy line/shot. The silence before the button
   is as important as the button.

## Techniques

- **Music/sound design lead.** Hits, risers, impacts, and a "braam" on title reveals.
  Edit picture to the sound design, not the other way around.
- **Beat-synced cuts in the montage** (use `analyze-music`); escalate cut speed.
- **Pre-drop silence / cut to black** for impact before the title.
- **Speed ramps** into hits. **Flash/whip** on big transitions (sparingly).
- **Text beats** (titles/quotes) land on downbeats; reveal in rhythm.
- **Withhold.** Show enough to intrigue, never the full payoff. Leave a question.
